Not every plumbing problem announces itself clearly as a leak, a clog, or a broken fixture, and general plumbing repair covers the calls that start with "something's wrong but I'm not sure what" as much as the ones with an obvious cause.

If A Pipe Has Burst Or Water Is Coming Through A Wall

A burst pipe floods fast. The goal in the first minutes is to stop the water and keep power away from it.

  1. Close the main shutoff and open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.
  2. Switch off the breaker for any room with water on the floor or coming through the ceiling.
  3. Move furniture and valuables away from the wet area and use towels or a wet vac to slow the spread.
  4. Call (770) 214-4545 and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from and whether it has stopped.

When General Plumbing Repair Is An Emergency

Water spraying from a pipe, a ceiling stain that keeps growing, a cracked fitting that will not stop dripping, and a home with no water at all are emergency calls. Smaller problems such as a slow drip or a sticking valve can usually wait for a scheduled visit.

When you are not sure which side of the line your problem falls on, call and describe it. The dispatcher can help sort an emergency from a repair that can wait until morning.

What Falls Under General Repair

A running toilet, a dripping faucet, a loose showerhead, a slow-draining tub, a water hammer sound in the walls, or a fixture that just stopped working all fall under general repair rather than one of the specialized service categories. Most of these trace back to a worn washer, a loose connection, or a failed cartridge, all fixable in a single visit.

Diagnostic-First Means No Guessing

A technician traces the actual cause before quoting a fix, since two problems that look identical from the symptom alone, like low water pressure at one faucet versus low pressure throughout the house, have completely different causes and completely different repairs.

Water Hammer And Other Less Obvious Issues

A loud banging noise in the walls right when a faucet or washing machine valve shuts off is called water hammer, caused by a shockwave when fast-moving water stops suddenly. It's usually fixed by installing or replacing water hammer arrestors near the affected fixture, a straightforward repair that stops what sounds like a much bigger problem.

An intermittent whistling sound from a fixture is often a partially closed valve or a worn washer creating turbulence, both quick fixes once identified.

Knowing which pattern applies before the visit shapes what a technician checks first.

A service call fee covers the initial diagnosis, and that fee is applied toward the repair cost if the work moves forward the same visit, so there is no double-charge for the trip itself.

When To Call Rather Than DIY

  • The problem involves the main water line or gas line, where a mistake risks a much larger failure than the original issue.
  • A repair attempt has already made the problem worse or introduced a second leak.
  • The issue is inside a wall, ceiling, or slab and would require cutting into a finished surface to access.
  • More than one fixture is affected at the same time, which usually points to a shared cause worth diagnosing properly rather than treating each symptom separately.

A problem that turns out to be a specific named issue gets routed to its own page for real depth: faucet and sink repair, toilet repair, or drain cleaning most commonly. Smyrna's mixed housing stock generates a similar range of general repair calls.

Cracked, Broken And Leaking Pipes

A cracked elbow, a split PVC connector or a pinhole in a half-inch copper line can spray or drip for days before anyone notices. The repair depends on the material and on where the pipe sits, whether in a wall, a garage or under a floor.

Technicians cut out the damaged section and install a new fitting or a short length of pipe. If the pipe is old and several spots are failing, replacing a longer run saves repeat visits, and the repiping page explains when that makes sense.

Low Water Pressure Or No Water At All

Weak pressure at every faucet points to a shutoff valve that is not fully open, a failing pressure regulator or corroded supply lines. Pressure that is low at only one fixture usually points to a clogged aerator or cartridge.

No water at all can mean a closed valve, a frozen or broken line, or an outage on the utility side. Check with your water utility for outages in the area, and call if the neighbors have water and you do not.

Leaks In Rentals, Condos And Multi-Unit Buildings

Landlords and property managers often call on behalf of tenants with a leak in a bathroom, a kitchen or a ceiling below. Giving the unit number, who has access and what the tenant noticed helps the dispatcher plan the visit.

Leaks in shared walls and stacks can affect more than one unit, so the technician looks for the source before repairing the visible damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency versus general repair?
An active leak, no water at all, or a sewage backup is an emergency. A slow drip, a running toilet, or a loose fixture is general repair and can typically be scheduled.
Do you charge extra just to diagnose the problem?
The diagnostic visit has a standard fee, which is applied toward the repair cost if you proceed with the fix that same visit.
Can you fix something a previous plumber or handyman started?
Yes, a technician will assess what's already been done and pick up from there rather than starting over unnecessarily.
Do you offer any kind of warranty on general repairs?
Yes, workmanship on repairs is warrantied. The specific term depends on the type of repair and is confirmed in writing before the work begins.
Can general repair calls turn into a bigger job on the spot?
Sometimes. If a technician finds a larger underlying issue during a routine repair, they'll explain it and provide a separate quote before doing any additional work.
Is it true that a small leak isn't worth fixing right away?
No. A small leak rarely stays small, and the cost of a delayed repair, including any water damage it eventually causes, is almost always higher than fixing it promptly.
How fast can a plumber reach me for a burst pipe in Kennesaw?
The line is staffed 24/7 and the dispatcher gives an arrival window based on current call volume. Closing the main shutoff while you wait limits the damage.
Can you repair a leaking jetted tub?
A technician can look at leaks at the jets, the pump connections and the drain. Parts depend on the make and model, so have those details ready when you call.
