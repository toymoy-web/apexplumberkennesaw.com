What We Can Verify About Legacy Park

Kennesaw's public subdivision map lists Legacy Park among the developments inside the city limits. Real-estate listing sites describe it as a golf community sitting between Interstate 75 and US Highway 41, close to Town Center at Cobb and Kennesaw State University, with walking trails, pools, tennis courts and a clubhouse. They also name several sections within it, including Lullwater, Highcroft and Winterthur at Legacy Park, and mention active adult (55+) options alongside single-family streets.

We treat listing sites as supporting evidence only. We do not quote home counts, prices or amenity lists here, because those change and the sites do not always agree with each other.

A Community Built In Sections Means Mixed Plumbing Ages

A neighborhood developed in phases is not one age. Homes in an early section and homes in a later section can be years apart, and they can have different fixtures, different water heaters and different repair histories even when they sit a few streets away from each other. For that reason we do not guess a pipe material from the neighborhood name. The year built on your county tax record, and what a technician actually sees under the sink and at the water heater, tell us far more.

As general plumbing information, not a Legacy Park finding: modern homes are usually plumbed with copper, PEX or CPVC supply lines and PVC or ABS drains, and the parts that wear out first are normally the moving ones. Water heaters, supply hoses, shutoff valves, toilet fill valves and faucet cartridges account for most calls on homes of that type.

Bigger Homes, Bigger Water Heater And Leak Questions

Larger houses tend to have longer pipe runs, more bathrooms and sometimes two water heaters. That has two practical effects. Hot water takes longer to reach the far bathroom, which is a recirculation or sizing question rather than a failure, and a slow leak can run a long time before anyone notices it, because the wet spot may be several rooms from the source. A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is the early signal. Our leak detection page explains how a leak is located without opening walls first.

If a tank is more than ten to twelve years old and starts to rumble, leak at the base or run out of hot water early, read our water heater installation and replacement page before it fails on its own schedule. Homeowners in an active adult section who would rather not carry or lift anything can also ask that the old unit be hauled away as part of the job.

Golf-Course And Common-Area Lots: Check Before You Dig

If your lot backs onto a fairway, trail or common area, any outdoor work needs a quick check first. The homeowners association may have rules about exterior work, and buried utility lines near the property edge are not always where you would expect. We would rather you confirm the water meter and shutoff locations, and your association's requirements, before a technician starts on a line outside the house.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve every section of Legacy Park?
Call (770) 214-4545 and give dispatch your street. We cover Kennesaw and the surrounding Cobb County area, including Legacy Park, and we confirm the address before anyone is sent.
Can you tell how old my plumbing is from the neighborhood?
Not reliably. Legacy Park was built in phases, so the year built on your tax record and what a technician sees on site are better guides than the community name.
Do I need HOA approval for plumbing work?
Interior repairs usually do not, but exterior work or anything affecting common areas might. Check your association's rules, and we can work around them.
My hot water is slow to reach a far bathroom. Is that a leak?
Not necessarily. In a large home it is often just distance, or a recirculation and sizing issue. A leak is more likely if your water bill has risen without a change in use.
