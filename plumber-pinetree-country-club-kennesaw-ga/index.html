Single-Family And Townhome Plumbing Are Different Jobs

In a detached house the plumbing is yours from the street meter to the roof vent. In a townhome the picture is shared. A supply line, a drain stack or a roof leak may serve more than one unit, and an association often owns part of the system. When a leak shows up in a townhome, the first practical question is where it starts and who owns that piece, and that answer can change who pays. It is worth reading your association documents before the first call. We will tell you what we find and where it is, so you can take it to the right place.

A Wide Range Of Ages In One Place

A community with homes from several decades means the same street can need different things. A newer townhome is more likely to need a water heater swap, a toilet valve or a faucet. An older detached house may have been partly repiped, partly not, and the question is what an inspection finds.
