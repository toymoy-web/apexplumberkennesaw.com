A slow drain, a gurgling sound from a nearby fixture, or water backing up into a tub when the washing machine runs are all signs of a clog worth clearing before it turns into a full backup, and same-day service is available across Kennesaw for exactly this reason.

If Your Drains Are Backing Up Right Now

A backup that is rising or spreading needs a call tonight, not tomorrow. These steps keep the problem from growing while the dispatcher sends a technician.

  1. Stop all water use. No showers, laundry or dishwasher until the line is cleared.
  2. Skip chemical drain cleaners. They can splash back on a technician and do not clear a main line blockage.
  3. Keep children and pets away from any water coming up through a floor drain, tub or shower.
  4. Call (770) 214-4545 and mention which fixtures back up and which ones still drain.

When A Clogged Drain Becomes An Emergency

One slow bathroom sink can wait for a regular appointment. Several fixtures backing up at once, water rising in the lowest tub or shower when the washer drains, gurgling toilets, or an overflowing floor drain point to a blocked main line.

A clog that has taken the only bathroom or the kitchen out of service also counts. Snaking handles most of these on the first visit, and hydro jetting is the next step when roots or heavy buildup keep bringing the blockage back.

What's Actually Causing The Clog

Kitchen drains clog from grease and food debris that solidifies inside the pipe as it cools, while bathroom drains clog from hair and soap buildup. Both are usually cleared with a standard drain snake in a single visit.

A clog that returns within a few weeks of snaking, especially on a main line, often means tree roots have worked into a joint in the sewer line rather than a simple blockage, which points toward hydro jetting or a camera inspection instead of repeated snaking.

Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

A mechanical snake physically breaks through a clog and pulls debris back, which works well for a single blockage point. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire interior wall of the pipe, removing grease buildup and root intrusion that a snake would just push past rather than clear.

A kitchen line with recurring grease buildup or a main line with root intrusion is usually a better hydro jetting candidate than a repeated snaking job.

Preventing The Next Clog

Pouring hot grease down a kitchen drain, even with the disposal running, is the single biggest cause of recurring kitchen clogs, since the grease cools and solidifies inside the pipe well before it reaches the street. A drain strainer catching hair before it enters a bathroom drain prevents most of the recurring bathroom clogs seen across Kennesaw homes.

For a home with a documented history of root intrusion, a maintenance hydro jetting visit every year or two keeps the line clear before it backs up again, which costs less over time than repeated emergency snaking calls.

Kennesaw's Older Homes See More Kitchen Line Clogs

Homes built before the 1990s near Kennesaw's older neighborhoods often have narrower-diameter kitchen drain lines than modern construction, which clog faster with the same amount of grease a newer, wider line would handle without issue. That narrower pipe diameter is worth knowing before assuming a persistent kitchen clog means something is uniquely wrong with the home.

Same-day service is available for both routine clogs and full backups, and a technician brings both snaking and jetting equipment to most calls so the right tool is available without a second visit if the first approach doesn't fully clear the line.

Clogged Drains From Big Shanty To Barrett Parkway

Slow drains have different usual causes across town. Older homes around downtown Big Shanty and Cherokee Street tend to have aging branch lines where scale and roots narrow the pipe, while homes and apartments along Barrett Parkway and Chastain Road more often clog from grease, hair, and wipes. Knowing which side of town the call comes from helps the technician choose between a cable, a camera, and a jetting pass.

What Not To Do Before We Arrive

  • Do not pour a chemical drain cleaner down a fully backed-up drain. It can splash back or damage older pipe.
  • Do not run additional water down the affected fixture, which can push a backup into a lower-level drain instead.
  • Do note which fixtures are affected, since more than one backing up at once usually means a main line issue, not a simple clog.

A clog that turns out to be root intrusion in the sewer line itself should see the sewer line repair page for what a spot repair or trenchless fix costs. Recurring clogs that keep coming back after snaking are exactly the pattern covered on the hydro jetting page. Mableton's mix of older and newer homes sees this same recurring-clog pattern.

Kitchen Sinks, Tubs And Showers: Where Clogs Start

Most single-fixture clogs build in a short stretch of pipe close to the fixture. Kitchen sinks collect grease, food and soap residue, while tubs and showers fill with hair and soap scum. These are usually cleared with a cable run from the fixture or the nearest cleanout.

A kitchen sink that backs up into the other basin or the dishwasher often points to a clog past the first branch. If one fixture clears but another still drains slowly, the blockage sits further down the line, and the technician tracks it back to the point where the lines join.

When The Snake Will Not Clear It

A cable that stops short, gets stuck, or comes back with roots or heavy buildup tells the technician the problem is more than a soft clog. Some homeowners have already tried a store-bought auger and left a broken piece behind.

The next step is a camera to see the pipe, followed by hydro jetting if the line is sound or a repair if it is not. The sewer line repair page covers the cases where the pipe itself has failed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a chemical drain cleaner before calling?
Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipes and make a mechanical clearing harder and messier for a technician. A call is safer for anything beyond a simple sink stopper clog.
How do I know if I need hydro jetting instead of snaking?
If the same drain clogs again within a few weeks of snaking, or if a kitchen line has a history of grease buildup, hydro jetting clears the problem more completely.
Is drain cleaning covered by homeowners insurance?
Routine drain clogs are considered maintenance and typically are not covered. Insurance may apply if a clog caused sudden water damage, which is a separate claim from the clearing service itself.
How often should drains be professionally cleaned as maintenance?
A kitchen line with heavy use benefits from cleaning every 1 to 2 years as prevention. Bathroom and other lines typically only need service when an actual clog develops.
Do you clear clogs in older cast iron drain lines?
Yes, both snaking and hydro jetting are used on cast iron, with jetting pressure adjusted appropriately for the pipe's age and condition after a camera check.
Can you clear a main line clog at night in Kennesaw?
Yes. Main line backups are handled on the 24/7 emergency line. The technician starts with cable equipment and moves to a camera or hydro jetting when the blockage is more than a simple clog.
Why does water back up in the tub when the washing machine drains?
The washer drain usually joins the main line below the tub. When that line is partly blocked, the washer water has nowhere to go and rises in the lowest fixture. It signals a main line problem and is worth clearing before it becomes a full backup.
Can you clear a floor drain, yard drain or driveway culvert pipe?
Floor drains and yard drains are routinely cleared with cable or jetting equipment. Driveway culverts depend on size and access, so describe the pipe when you call.
