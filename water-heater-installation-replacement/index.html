Ready For A New Water Heater?

☎ Call Now

A water heater that has stopped heating, is leaking at the base, or is simply past its expected 10 to 12 year service life is usually a replacement call rather than a repair call, and Kennesaw Apex Plumbing installs same-day in most cases across Kennesaw.

Signs It's Time To Replace, Not Repair

Rust-colored water from every hot tap, a tank that is visibly rusting on the outside, or a unit that has already needed two repairs in the past year are the clearest signals that replacement is the better math. A tank installed before 2014 is also old enough that exact replacement parts are getting harder to source.

A puddle forming at the base of the tank almost always means the tank itself has started to fail internally, and no repair fixes a cracked tank.

Tank vs. Tankless

A traditional tank unit costs less upfront and is a straightforward swap in most Kennesaw homes. A tankless unit costs more to install, especially if it requires new gas line sizing or electrical service, but produces continuous hot water and lasts longer, typically 15 to 20 years against a tank's 10 to 12.

Homes with slab foundations and a garage-mounted water heater, common across Kennesaw, are usually straightforward candidates for either option, since the vent and gas line routing rarely needs major rework.

Getting The Size Right

A tank sized too small runs out of hot water during back-to-back showers, while an oversized tank wastes energy keeping water hot that never gets used. Sizing is based on the home's peak-hour demand, generally a 40-gallon tank for 1-2 bathrooms and a 50-gallon tank for 3 or more, adjusted for household size.

A tankless unit is sized differently, by flow rate (gallons per minute) rather than storage capacity, and needs to be matched to how many fixtures might run hot water simultaneously in the home.

Where Kennesaw Water Heaters Usually Sit

Most Kennesaw homes on slab foundations keep the water heater in the garage, which simplifies venting for a gas unit but exposes the tank to wider temperature swings than an interior closet would, slightly shortening its working life. A garage installation also means any leak drains toward the garage floor rather than into living space, which is worth factoring in when deciding between tank and tankless for a specific home.

A call typically starts with confirming the unit's age, fuel type, and rough location so the technician arrives with the most likely-needed unit already on the truck, keeping most installations to a same-day, single-visit job rather than a return trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does water heater installation take?
Most tank replacements are completed in 2 to 3 hours. A tankless conversion that requires new gas line sizing can take a full day.
Do you haul away the old water heater?
Yes, haul-away of the old unit is included in every installation.
Is a permit required for water heater replacement in Georgia?
Yes, water heater replacement is governed by the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and International Fuel Gas Code as adopted with Georgia's state amendments, and a permit is typically required for the swap.
Can you install a water heater in a different location than the old one?
Yes, though relocating a unit adds cost for extending gas, electrical, and venting to the new spot, and any relocation still needs to meet the same code clearances as the original installation.
What brands do you install and repair?
Rheem, A.O. Smith, Rinnai, State, and Bradford White are commonly stocked, covering the large majority of units found in Kennesaw homes.
