Privacy Policy

How Kennesaw Apex Plumbing handles the information you share with us.

This policy explains what information Kennesaw Apex Plumbing collects when you visit this website or contact us, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

This website does not use contact forms. The only information we receive directly from a visitor is what's shared over the phone or by email when you contact us, typically your name, phone number, address, and a description of the plumbing issue, which we use solely to schedule and perform the requested service.

Like most websites, this site's hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

How We Use Information

Information collected during a phone call or email is used only to schedule, dispatch, and invoice the service requested. We do not sell, rent, or share customer contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies for functionality and to understand aggregate visitor traffic. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Links

This site may link to third-party services, such as Google Maps for location embeds. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review separately.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information shared with us during the course of providing service, though no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices. The date of the most recent update is noted below.

Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to contact@apexplumberkennesaw.com or by calling (770) 214-4545.
