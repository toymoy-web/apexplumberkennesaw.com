If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Marietta

In the oldest Marietta houses near the Square the main shutoff is often an aging gate valve in a basement or crawl space that has not been turned in years, so locate it before the emergency rather than during it.

  1. Close the main shutoff, which is a ball valve at the meter box on newer homes and often a stiff gate valve inside the basement or crawl space on older homes near the Square.
  2. Switch off power to any wet room from the breaker panel if the water is near an outlet, and move rugs and boxes off the floor.
  3. Call (770) 214-4545. A Marietta call after midnight goes to the same dispatch line as a daytime call, and the crew leaves from Kennesaw, only about nine miles away.

Marietta's Oldest Pipes Sit Near The Square

Homes in and around Marietta Square include some of the oldest houses in Cobb County, and many were plumbed with galvanized steel supply lines and cast iron drains that are now well past a normal service life. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside, which shows up as falling pressure, rust-tinted water after a night of no use, or a fitting that weeps where the threads have thinned.

A mix of historic homes in the blocks around Marietta Square, mid-century ranches across the older neighborhoods, and newer subdivisions on the side toward Kennesaw Mountain. In the mid-century ranches that fill much of West Marietta and East Marietta, copper supply lines are common, but the cast iron or clay drain beneath the slab or crawl space is often original and is where the next failure usually starts.

The ground here is the same Cobb County red clay found in Kennesaw, which swells with rain and shrinks in dry spells and moves buried pipe joints a little each year. The mature oaks and maples that make Marietta's older streets so attractive also send roots toward any joint in a clay or cast iron sewer line. That is why Sewer Line Repair is the most requested service in the city and why a camera inspection comes before any digging.

Two Water Systems And A Hillside Between Them

Depending on the address, water in Marietta can come from Marietta's city utility or the Cobb County Water System depending on the address. A technician who works both knows to confirm which meter and which shutoff applies before turning anything, which matters at 2 a.m. when the first job is simply finding the right valve.

Marietta's older streets are lined with mature trees and sit on rolling clay hills that send runoff downhill after every heavy storm. The streets that climb toward the Marietta side of Kennesaw Mountain National Battlefield Park shed that runoff to the lots below them. A Leak Detection call there often traces to a wet crawl space or a buried irrigation line instead of a failing house pipe.

Marietta also has a large rental and student population around Kennesaw State's Marietta campus and Life University, where heavy daily use means fixture and water heater wear tends to show up sooner than in an owner-occupied house. Coverage also reaches the 30152 ZIP code on the Kennesaw side, 30127 toward Powder Springs, and 30080 and 30082 toward Smyrna for homes near those borders.
